# 01 — Product

**Status:** PROPOSED

## Purpose

What Divido is, who it is for, what the proposed MVP includes and excludes, and which features are deferred. Everything in this section is a proposal, and none of it is frozen, until the project owner approves it (see [requirements freeze](../00-governance/requirements-freeze.md)).

## Documents

- [Product overview](product-overview.md): vision, users, and problem.
- [Requirements](requirements.md): product requirement register (`REQ-*`, `NFR-*`).
- [MVP scope](mvp-scope.md): proposed included and excluded scope.
- [Scope and decisions](scope-and-decisions.md): supporting summary of scope and open product decisions.
- [Future features](future-features.md): features deferred beyond the MVP, including `FUT-LEND-001` Money Lending Tracker.
- [Glossary](glossary.md): shared terminology.

## Decision records

Decisions are recorded in the canonical [decision log](../00-governance/decision-log.md). [Scope and decisions](scope-and-decisions.md) summarizes scope and open questions for product readers, but it is not a separate register. Where the two differ, the decision log is authoritative.
