# MVP Scope

**Status:** PROPOSED — not frozen

## Included in MVP

### Account and setup
- Register, sign in, sign out.
- Secure password hashing and server-side session handling.
- Profile preferences and guided onboarding.
- Initial language: English.
- Proposed defaults: currency `LKR`, timezone `Asia/Colombo`.

### Income and expenses
- Multiple income sources such as salary, freelance, business, rental income, and other.
- Record actual income against an income source.
- Quick expense entry requiring amount and category.
- Optional description, payment method, and editable date.
- Transaction history, filtering, editing, soft deletion, and restoration.

### Categories and budgeting
- Built-in categories mapped to Needs, Wants, Culture, Unexpected.
- User-created categories.
- Monthly planned income and savings target.
- Independent monthly budgets for the four Kakeibo groups.
- Warnings when planned group limits plus savings target exceed planned income; exact wording/calculation remains to be specified.

### Dashboard and insights
- Monthly planned and actual income.
- Actual expenses and net recorded savings.
- Group budget versus actual spending.
- Spending-habit insights over a proposed 90-day period, requiring at least three distinct spending days for a candidate pattern.
- Monthly reflection.

## Out of MVP

- Sending money through Divido.
- Bank or payment-provider integrations.
- Shared expenses and household collaboration.
- Money lending tracker, person search by username/phone, loan repayments, and outstanding balances.
- Automatic recurring transactions and scheduled payments.
- Multiple bank/wallet account balances.
- Sinhala UI and full localization.

## Scope control

Anything not listed as included should be treated as out of MVP until approved through the decision/change process. The final list must be reconciled with all stories and API contracts before freeze.
